What remains when the work is done?

A finished file holds the result. The attempts, revisions, and decisions that shaped it are often invisible. Provenance makes the recorded process available for examination.

Demo dynamic

How it works

Same file. Two histories.

Identical code can emerge through different paths. One unfolds over hours of edits and tests. Another arrives in a single paste.

I. Continuity

Every event is connected.

Changes, pastes, and saves form a hash-linked record, preserving the sequence of recorded activity.

II. Integrity

The past leaves traces.

Alter an event, and the hashes that follow no longer match the original chain.

III. Verification

Trust, made verifiable.

Signed checkpoints anchor the history. A rebuilt chain alone cannot reproduce an authentic signature.

    For students

    The work is yours. So is its history.

    Your edits and revisions deserve a record. Provenance preserves supported development activity without watching the rest of your digital life.

    Recorded

    • Edits and pastes in the assignment workspace
    • File activity and editor focus
    • Supported terminal commands
    • Installed editor extensions

    Never recorded

    • Your browser, screen, camera, or other projects
    • System-level keystrokes
    • Clipboard contents unless pasted into your work
    • Offline. No network requests while recording.
    • Readable. Plain-text logs stay local until submission.
    • Course-defined retention. Your school sets the deletion schedule.

    For instructors & TAs

    Look beyond the submission.

    A final file shows what was handed in, not how it came to be. Provenance brings the recorded development history into view, so review can begin with evidence rather than assumptions.

    Analyzer / Review demonstration

    The moments between the answers.

    A failed test. A discarded approach. A function written again. The replay reveals recorded attempts and revisions that the finished program leaves behind.

    Tools

    One record. Every tool.

    From editor to submission to review, Provenance connects an open-source workflow around the recorded history.

    Record

    provcode Provenance for VS Code Records supported development activity in VS Code. provjet Provenance for JetBrains Records activity in compatible JetBrains IDEs. provnvim Provenance for Neovim A lightweight recorder for Neovim.

    Hand in

    Signed bundle The history and its signed evidence, packaged for submission.
    provgate Gradescope gateway Transfers new submissions to the Provenance server.

    Check

    provd Provenance server Receives submissions and verifies the available evidence.

    Review

    provlens Provenance analyzer Explore the events, signals, and recorded development history.

      What a record cannot know.

      A log captures events, not intention. It cannot guarantee authorship or make forgery impossible.

      Provenance makes certain changes detectable and the recorded process examinable. The evidence still needs a human reader.
